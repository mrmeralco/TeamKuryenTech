Why SmartWatts

Smart meters and wired sensors are expensive and intrusive. SmartWatts uses what you already have — a phone camera and your monthly bill — plus passive NFC cards and QR codes for rentals.

Households

Understand the bill, find the appliances driving it, and see what a habit change is worth before you make it.

Tenants

Tap your NFC card on your phone to see your fair share of the bill — while your appliance list stays yours alone.

Landlords

Upload one master bill, set up shared or individual sub-meters, and end the monthly back-and-forth over who owes what.

Point, shoot, done. The photo is gone in seconds.

Know what a habit is worth before you change it.

Drag a slider — say, two fewer aircon hours a day — and SmartWatts recomputes your bill on Meralco's 8-item formula to show the exact kWh and peso difference.
